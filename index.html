Our story

It started as a secret family recipe.

Marsel spent years tweaking one recipe in a tiny kitchen, handing out unlabeled bottles to friends and family. Then the friends started asking for refills. Then their friends started asking. Then strangers.

So we made it official. Every batch of Marsel's Special Sauce is still made in small runs, with real chiles and zero shortcuts, the same way it was the very first time.

Hot questions

How hot is Marsel's Special Sauce?

It's a medium heat, about a 6 out of 10. You get the flavor first, then a warm kick that builds without wrecking your taste buds.

What do you put Marsel's Special Sauce on?

Everything. Fans' favorites are eggs, tacos, wings, pizza, burgers, mac and cheese, rice bowls and Bloody Marys.

Do I need to refrigerate it after opening?

It's shelf stable, but refrigerating after opening keeps the color bright and the flavor fresh longest.

How fast does it ship?

Orders ship within 2 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $35 in the United States.

Is it gluten free and vegan?

Yes. Marsel's Special Sauce is made with all-natural ingredients and is gluten free and vegan, with no added sugar or preservatives.
