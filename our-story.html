Our Story

From one tiny kitchen to your table.

Marsel's Special Sauce didn't start as a business plan. It started as a pot on the stove, a family recipe, and a lot of people asking, "Wait — can I get more of that?"

Made by Marsel not a factory

How it happened

A sauce in five chapters

  1. 🍲

    Chapter 1

    The recipe

    It began in Marsel's kitchen with a handful of red chiles, a head of garlic and a stubborn belief that hot sauce should taste like something, not just burn.

  2. 🧪

    Chapter 2

    A hundred tiny tweaks

    A little more lime. A little less vinegar. Roast the garlic first. Batch after batch, the recipe got closer to the one in Marsel's head.

  3. 🫙

    Chapter 3

    The unlabeled bottles

    Friends and family got bottles with no label, just a piece of tape that said "Marsel's." They came back empty. Fast.

  4. 📣

    Chapter 4

    The refill requests

    Then friends of friends started asking. Then people Marsel had never met. That's when the "special sauce" became Marsel's Special Sauce.

  5. 🏠

    Chapter 5

    Your kitchen

    Today every bottle is still made in small batches, the same way as the very first one, and shipped straight to you. The next chapter is yours.

Meet the maker

Hi, I'm Marsel.

I've always been the person who brings their own hot sauce to dinner. Most of what I found was either all fire and no flavor, or all flavor and no fun. So I started making my own.

I still taste every batch before it goes out. If it's not something I'd pour on my own eggs in the morning, it doesn't get a label.

"Flavor first. Fire second. And always, always put it on everything."

What we believe

The Sauce Code

👅

Flavor first

Heat should make food better, not hide it. Every batch is balanced for taste before burn.

🌶️

Real ingredients

Chiles, garlic, vinegar, lime, salt. If you can't pronounce it, it's not in the bottle.

🥄

Small batches

We make it in small runs so every bottle tastes just like the first one did.

🤝

Made for sharing

This sauce got its start being passed around a table. That's still the whole point.

From chile to bottle

How every batch is made

  1. 1

    Pick the chiles

    Ripe red Fresno and cayenne chiles, chosen for flavor as much as heat.

  2. 2

    Roast the garlic

    Slow-roasted until sweet and mellow. This is where the magic starts.

  3. 3

    Blend & simmer

    Everything comes together with vinegar, fresh lime and that one secret ingredient.

  4. 4

    Bottle & taste

    Every batch is taste-tested before it's bottled, labeled and sent your way.
