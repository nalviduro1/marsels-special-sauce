Oven-Crispy Hot Wings

Shatteringly crisp wings with no deep fryer, tossed in a buttery Marsel's sauce. The secret is baking powder — it dries the skin so it crackles in the oven.

  • ⏱️ 60 min
  • 🍽️ Serves 4
  • 🌶️🌶️

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken wings, split into flats and drums
  • 1 tbsp baking powder (not baking soda)
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ⅓ cup Marsel's Special Sauce
  • 4 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp honey

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Pat the wings very dry and toss with baking powder and salt.
  2. Arrange on a rack over a foil-lined sheet pan. Bake 25 min, flip, then 20–25 min more until deep golden.
  3. Whisk Marsel's, melted butter and honey in a big bowl.
  4. Toss the hot wings in the sauce and serve right away.

Spicy Honey Butter

Sweet, salty, spicy. Slather it on cornbread, biscuits, grilled corn or fried chicken.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp Marsel's Special Sauce
  • Pinch of flaky salt

Steps

  1. Mash everything together with a fork until smooth.
  2. Spread on anything warm. Keeps 1 week in the fridge.

Hot Honey Pepperoni Pizza

Crispy-edged pepperoni pizza finished with a sticky sweet-heat drizzle.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb pizza dough
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 cup pepperoni
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp Marsel's Special Sauce

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 475°F. Stretch the dough onto an oiled sheet pan.
  2. Top with sauce, cheese and pepperoni. Bake 10–12 min until bubbly and browned.
  3. Warm the honey, stir in Marsel's and drizzle over the hot pizza.

Saucy Breakfast Tacos

Soft scrambled eggs, melty cheese and avocado, finished with plenty of dashes.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 4 small tortillas, warmed
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar or Jack
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Cilantro
  • Marsel's Special Sauce, to taste

Steps

  1. Whisk eggs with a pinch of salt. Scramble gently in butter over low heat until just set.
  2. Fill tortillas with eggs, cheese, avocado and cilantro.
  3. Finish each taco with 3–4 dashes of Marsel's.
🧀
  • 25 min

Kicked-Up Mac & Cheese

Creamy stovetop mac with Marsel's stirred right into the cheese sauce.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 8 oz elbow pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1½ cups whole milk
  • 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar
  • 1–2 tbsp Marsel's Special Sauce

Steps

  1. Cook pasta in salted water until just tender. Drain.
  2. Melt butter in the pot, whisk in flour for 1 min, then slowly whisk in milk. Simmer until thick.
  3. Off the heat, stir in cheese, then Marsel's. Fold in the pasta and salt to taste.
🥔
  • 40 min

Crispy Spicy Potatoes

Golden roasted baby potatoes tossed in hot sauce butter. Dangerously snackable.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp Marsel's Special Sauce
  • Chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with oil and salt; roast cut-side down 30–35 min.
  2. Toss with melted butter, Marsel's and parsley.

Spicy Michelada

Cold beer, lime, chili salt and Marsel's. Patio season in a glass.

Get the recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lime
  • Chili-lime salt for the rim
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 4–6 dashes Marsel's Special Sauce
  • 2 dashes Worcestershire
  • 12 oz cold Mexican lager

Steps

  1. Rub a lime wedge around a tall glass rim and dip in chili-lime salt. Fill with ice.
  2. Add lime juice, Marsel's and Worcestershire. Top slowly with beer and stir gently.
