Shipping questions

Do you ship internationally?

Right now we ship to all 50 US states, including APO/FPO addresses. International shipping is on our list — join the Sauce Squad to hear first.

Can I change my address or order after I place it?

Yes, if it hasn't shipped yet. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we'll update it.

What if my package is lost or arrives broken?

Send us your order number and a photo within 30 days and we'll ship a free replacement. You don't need to send anything back.

Can I return an opened bottle?

You don't need to. If you don't love it, tell us within 30 days and we'll refund or replace it under our happiness guarantee.
