Wholesale questions

Is there a minimum wholesale order?

Opening orders start at two cases of 12 bottles, and reorders can be as small as one case.

Can we try samples first?

Yes. Once your application is approved we'll send free samples so you and your team can taste it first.

Where do you ship wholesale orders?

We ship wholesale orders anywhere in the United States, usually within 3–5 business days.

Do you offer food-service sizes for restaurants?

Yes. Restaurants can order table-ready bottles or ask us about bulk sizes for the kitchen.
